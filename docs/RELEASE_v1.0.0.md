# 🎉 LuminaBox v1.0.0 (流光音匣)

Welcome to the very first official release of **LuminaBox**! 
欢迎来到 **流光音匣 (LuminaBox)** 的首个正式版本发布！

This mod is designed to completely redefine your Minecraft auditory experience by giving you full control over what plays in the background, without ever needing to minimize the game.

这款模组旨在彻底重新定义你的 Minecraft 听觉体验。无需切出游戏，你便能完全掌控游戏内的背景音乐。

---

## ✨ Key Features (核心功能)

- **🎨 In-Game Music Player (游戏内音乐面板):** 
  A sleek, draggable, and scalable GUI accessible directly in-game. Control playback, manage playlists, and tweak volume natively.
  *一个精美、可拖拽、自动缩放的游戏内界面，让你无缝控制音乐播放、管理播放列表及调整音量。*

- **📂 Local Audio Support (本地音频支持):** 
  Import and play your favorite MP3 and audio files from your computer directly into your Minecraft world.
  *直接将电脑里的本地音频文件添加到你的 Minecraft 世界中播放。*

- **🌐 Online Streaming (网络流媒体播放):** 
  Natively search and stream music from Bilibili using our integrated Lavaplayer framework. Just search, add, and listen!
  *内置 Lavaplayer 框架，支持直接在游戏内搜索并播放 Bilibili 的网络音乐。随搜随听！*

- **🌍 Environmental Triggers (环境触发系统):** 
  Configure custom background music that triggers automatically based on your surroundings, biomes, or events.
  *根据不同的地形群系、环境或服务器事件，自动触发并播放对应的背景音乐。*

- **🔇 Vanilla Override (原版音乐替换):** 
  Toggle the option to completely silence and replace the vanilla Minecraft background music with your own curated tracks.
  *支持一键开启替换模式，覆盖并静音原版的背景音乐。*

- **🌐 Network Proxy (内置网络代理设置):** 
  Built-in HTTP proxy settings allow you to resolve network timeouts when playing streaming music from certain online platforms.
  *内置 HTTP 网络代理设置区，帮你解决网络环境导致的 B站 等流媒体播放超时问题。*

- **🌍 Multi-Language (多语言支持):** 
  Fully localized into **9 languages**, including English, 简体中文, 繁体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Français, Español, العربية, and Português.
  *全面支持 9 种语言（含简繁中、英、日、韩、法、西、阿、葡）。*

---

## 📥 Installation (安装说明)

**Supported Minecraft Version (支持的我的世界版本):** `1.21.x`
**Supported Loaders (支持的加载器):** `Fabric` & `NeoForge`

1. Download the `.jar` file matching your mod loader. / 下载对应加载器的 `.jar` 文件。
2. Drop it into your `.minecraft/mods` folder. / 将其放入 `.minecraft/mods` 文件夹。
3. Launch the game and press the designated hotkey to open the LuminaBox GUI! / 启动游戏，按下对应的按键或点击图标呼出流光音匣面板！
